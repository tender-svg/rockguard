Pipeline Protection Mesh

RockGuard® HD

RockGuard® HD Pipeline Protection Mesh is an 11 mm, three dimensional bi-planar extruded diamond structured mesh rock shield manufactured from polyethylene, providing long-term protection to pipelines during backfill operations, rock migration, and even during future excavation.

Geotextile Buoyancy Bags

PipeWeights

PipeWeights is a breathable, woven polypropylene geotextile bag designed as a buoyancy control feature in the pipeline industry. It is filled with aggregate and placed on the pipeline to provide long-term buoyancy control.

Installation & Jobsite

Accessories

RockGuard® Tape is a 3/4" filament tape made for installing RockGuard® products. It makes RockGuard® HD easy to install — simply wrap it circumferentially around the pipe every 6".

PipeWeights Hoppers make it easy to pour aggregate into PipeWeights bags. Hoppers are available for jobsite rental, and we also offer on-site filling services and delivery to the lay down yard or right-of-way.
