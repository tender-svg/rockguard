About the Manufacturer

Trusted by the Pipeline Industry Since 1981

Industrial Fabrics, Inc. has provided trusted service to the pipeline industry since 1981, and manufactures RockGuard® HD Pipeline Protection Mesh — the standard in pipeline asset protection.

RockGuard® HD is an 11 mm, three-dimensional bi-planar extruded diamond mesh rock shield made from polyethylene. It protects pipe coating during backfill, rock migration and future excavation, and stays lightweight and flexible to handle — even at low temperatures.

Today RockGuard® HD holds over 75 midstream approvals and more than 45 contractor approvals.

Geotextile Buoyancy Bags

PipeWeights

This breathable, woven polypropylene geotextile bag is designed as a buoyancy control feature for the pipeline industry. PipeWeights is filled with aggregate and placed on the pipeline to provide long term buoyancy control and restricts uplift without damaging the pipe's coating.
